Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

AI Gallery Cleaner (the “App”) is published by Habibe Atik (“we”, “us”), Istanbul, Türkiye, who acts as the data controller for the limited information described in this policy. Contact: appsroutine@gmail.com.

2. Summary

The App is designed to analyze your photo library on your device. We do not operate servers for the App, and the App is not designed to upload your photos, videos or analysis results to us. The App does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Photo library access and on-device analysis

  • To find copies, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos, the App asks for access to your photo library. You can allow full access or limit it to selected items, and you can change this at any time in your device settings.
  • The analysis runs on your device using Apple’s frameworks, such as Photos, Vision (including on-device machine-learning features built into iOS), Core Image and AVFoundation.
  • Previews used for the analysis are requested from the photos stored on your device. When you play a video whose original is stored only in iCloud, Apple’s Photos framework may download it from your iCloud account; this transfer is handled by Apple, and we do not receive the video.

4. Information stored on your device

  • Analysis results. To avoid scanning your whole library again on every launch, the App keeps an index in its private storage on your device. It may include identifiers of library items, their dates, sizes and types, quality measurements, small numeric descriptions derived from the images that are used to compare them, and the resulting groups and suggestions. It is not intended to include copies of your photos or videos.
  • Settings and counters. App settings, the number of free deletions used today and the date of your last cleanup are stored on your device.
  • Widget. If you add the App’s widget, a small summary (numbers, storage sizes and a date, without photos) is stored in a container on your device that the widget can read.
  • Backups. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include the App’s data in that backup. Backups are provided by Apple under your account, and we do not have access to them.
  • Deleting the App removes the App’s data from your device, subject to how iOS handles backups.

5. Deleting and replacing items

When you choose to delete items or to replace a video with a smaller copy, the App asks Apple’s Photos framework to make the change, and iOS asks you to confirm it. Deleted items usually move to the “Recently Deleted” album, as managed by Apple. We do not receive copies of your photos or videos. Smaller copies of videos are created on your device and saved to your photo library.

6. Reminders and background processing

  • If you turn on the optional monthly reminder, the App asks for permission to show notifications and schedules a local reminder on your device. No push server of ours is involved. You can turn it off in the App or in your device settings.
  • If you choose to continue a scan in the background, iOS may run the analysis on your device while the App is not open and shows its progress. You can stop it at any time.

7. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details, billing address or Apple Account password. The App checks your purchase status with Apple on your device. Apple’s privacy policy applies to payments: apple.com/legal/privacy.

8. Ratings

The App may occasionally show Apple’s standard rating prompt or open the App Store so you can write a review. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple.

9. Optional diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with crash and performance information about the App, managed according to Apple’s privacy practices. This information is not intended to include your photos or videos. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

10. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to share, and we use it to respond to your request. Emails are handled through our email service provider, which may store them on its servers in other countries. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed for support and reasonable record-keeping.

Please avoid sending photos or sensitive personal information by email. You can ask us to access or delete your information, subject to applicable law.

11. Information we do not intend to collect

The App is not designed to collect your photos, videos or analysis results, your contacts, location, browsing history, advertising identifiers or usage analytics, and we do not build advertising profiles. We do not sell or rent personal information.

12. Permissions

The App may ask for access to your photo library (to analyze it and, when you choose, to delete items or save smaller copies of videos) and for permission to show notifications (only if you turn on the monthly reminder). You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

13. Why we use this information

We use the information in support emails only to answer you and to keep reasonable records of our correspondence. Diagnostics are collected and shared by Apple based on the choice you make in your device settings.

14. Retention

  • On-device data: kept until you delete it or delete the App.
  • Support emails: as long as needed for support and reasonable record-keeping, unless you ask us to delete them (subject to applicable law).

15. Security

Because the analysis takes place on your device, your content is protected by the security features of your device and iOS, such as device encryption and your passcode. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

To exercise these rights, contact appsroutine@gmail.com. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Data kept by the App stays on your device, so you can remove it yourself at any time by deleting the App.
