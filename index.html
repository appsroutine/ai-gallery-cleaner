AI Gallery Cleaner

Free up space in your photo library on your iPhone. The App helps you find copies, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos, suggests a best shot to keep, and leaves every decision to you. Photos are analyzed on your device.

Nothing is deleted without your confirmation, and deleted items usually stay in Recently Deleted for about 30 days.
