# Privacy Policy for **AI Gallery Cleaner**
_Last updated: October 2025_

**AI Gallery Cleaner** (“App”, “we”, “us”, “our”) helps you analyze and clean your local photo & video library.  
We respect your privacy and minimize data processing by design.

---

## 1. What We Do (Plain-English Summary)
- The App **analyzes photos and videos locally on your device** to find similar, blurry, screenshots and large items.
- You decide what to delete. Deletions are performed through Apple’s Photos framework and items are moved to **Recently Deleted** (Apple’s behavior).
- The developer does **not** run any external servers for your media. We **do not upload** your photos/videos to our servers.

---

## 2. Data We Process

### 2.1 On-Device Processing (No External Upload)
- Content analysis (similarity, blur scoring, classification) is executed **on device** using Apple frameworks (Photos, Vision/Core Image) and/or on-device ML.
- We may read **metadata** that Photos provides (e.g., creation date, resolution, media type) to display summaries and estimates.
- **We do not collect biometric identifiers** and we do not build any user profiles for advertising.

### 2.2 Optional Diagnostic Data
- Crash logs and performance metrics may be shared with us **only if** you opt in to Apple’s “Share with App Developers”.  
  These logs are de-identified and controlled by Apple. We do not receive your photos or contacts via diagnostics.

### 2.3 Contact & Support
- If you email us at **appsroutine@gmail.com**, we will see your email address and any information you include.  
  We use this solely to respond and then retain for reasonable record-keeping. You may request deletion (see Section 8).

---

## 3. What We **Do Not** Collect
- We do **not** collect your photos, videos, face data, precise geolocation, financial data, or contacts on our servers.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or trade personal data.
- We do **not** conduct cross-app tracking.

---

## 4. Photos & Deletions
- When you delete items, the App instructs the Photos framework to move them to **Recently Deleted**.  
  Apple permanently removes those items after Apple’s retention window unless you restore them in the Photos app.
- The App itself **does not keep recoverable copies** outside of Photos.
- Any temporary thumbnails generated for previews are kept **only on device** and may be purged by the system or when you uninstall.

---

## 5. Advertising (AdMob) & Consent

### 5.1 Free + Ads / Premium + No Ads
- The free tier shows ads via **Google AdMob**. Premium removes ads and may unlock features.
- All ad serving is subject to **GDPR**, **CCPA**, **ATT** (App Tracking Transparency), and **Google UMP** consent flows.

### 5.2 Personalization Controls
- In EEA/UK: we display the **Google UMP** consent form. Your choice determines ad personalization.  
- If you deny tracking in **ATT**, ad networks must serve **non-personalized**/contextual ads.
- **We do not directly access or store** Google’s advertising identifiers or user-level ad profiles.

### 5.3 Third-Party Responsibility & Safeguard
- Ad requests and any related identifiers are processed by **Google** under its own privacy policy.  
- While we implement the official SDKs and consent controls, **we cannot technically audit Google’s servers**.
- **Fallback Safety:** If a misconfiguration or third-party error results in unintended personalized ads, the App will attempt to **force non-personalized requests** until the issue is resolved.

> **Disclosure:** Clicking ads may take you to third-party websites/apps. We are **not responsible** for external content or practices.

---

## 6. In-App Purchases
- Payments are handled by Apple. We do **not** receive your credit card, Apple ID, or billing details.
- Purchase history is stored by Apple; the App reads only the entitlement to unlock features.

---

## 7. Legal Bases & Roles

- For EEA/UK users, where applicable, our legal bases include **Consent** (ads) and **Legitimate Interests** (basic app functionality such as counting items, showing storage).
- For ads, **Google** is a separate controller of any data it processes for ad delivery. We are **not** a controller or processor of Google’s ad-profile data.

---

## 8. Your Choices & Data Rights

- **Permissions:** You can change Photos access and Notifications in iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security**.  
- **Ads Choices:** Reset advertising identifiers and adjust tracking preferences in **Settings**.  
- **Access/Deletion Requests (support data):** Email **appsroutine@gmail.com**. We’ll verify and respond within a reasonable time.
- **Uninstall:** Removing the App deletes local app data. Items already deleted through Photos follow Apple’s **Recently Deleted** policy.

---

## 9. Children’s Privacy
- The App is intended for **users 16+**. We do not knowingly collect data from children.  
  If you believe a child provided us data via support email, contact us to delete it.

---

## 10. Security
- On-device data benefits from iOS security, including file-system encryption.  
- Our App ID uses Apple **Data Protection** (Complete Protection).  
- We follow the principle of **data minimization** and do not operate external media servers.

---

## 11. International & Regulatory
- We aim to comply with **GDPR (EU/UK)**, **CCPA/CPRA (California)**, and **KVKK (Türkiye)** to the extent applicable.  
- Because we do not externally store your media, the App typically qualifies as **local-processing**.  
- If future features require remote processing or cloud sync, we will update this Policy and obtain fresh consent where required.
